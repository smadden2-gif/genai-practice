Why context control matters

Helpful AI should not mean context without consent.

People often have useful details in separate work and personal tools. An assistant that ignores those details forces repetition. One that pulls them all in without asking creates a different problem.

Context Bridge explores a middle path: bring the right context to a task with your permission, and make the boundary visible.
